How to Write a YouTube Title That Gets Clicks Honestly

Trenndly Blog · 5 min read · Updated November 2026

Your title and thumbnail work together as a promise to the viewer. A good title makes the right person curious, and your video then keeps that promise. Here is how to write titles that do both.

Keep the length in mind

YouTube allows titles up to 100 characters, but search results and phones often cut a title after roughly 60 to 70 characters. Put the most important words at the start so nothing important is hidden.

Put the keyword early

Think about what a viewer would type to find your video, and use that phrase near the beginning. A viewer should understand the topic in one glance.

Add a reason to click

A keyword alone is often dull. Add one element that creates interest:

  • A specific result: "Jaisalmer in 3 Days: Full Budget Plan and Real Costs".
  • A number: "7 Mistakes New Photographers Make".
  • A question the viewer already has: "Is a Used Laptop Worth It in 2026?"
  • A clear benefit: "Edit Videos Faster With 5 Simple Shortcuts".

Weak vs better titles

  • Weak: "My Trip" → Better: "Udaipur Trip on a Budget: 2 Days, Rs 4,000".
  • Weak: "Cooking Video 12" → Better: "Easy Paneer Butter Masala Recipe in 20 Minutes".

Do not mislead

If the title promises something the video does not deliver, viewers leave early. A low watch time tells YouTube that people were not satisfied. Clickbait can give a small spike and then hurt your channel. Make the title exciting, but always true.

Test and learn

After a few days, check YouTube Studio for impressions click-through rate and average view duration. If people see your video but do not click, the title or thumbnail may need work. If they click but leave quickly, the opening of the video may need work. Try writing five different titles for each video before choosing one, and pick the clearest.

Checklist

  • Main keyword in the first half.
  • Around 60 characters or less when possible.
  • One clear reason to click.
  • The video really delivers what the title says.
