How to Write YouTube Tags That Actually Help

Trenndly Blog · 5 min read · Updated November 2026

Tags are keywords you add in the "Show more" section when you upload a video. Viewers do not see them on the watch page, so many creators wonder whether tags matter at all. Here is an honest answer and a simple way to write them well.

What tags really do

YouTube has said that tags play only a minor role in how your video is found. Their main use is to help the system understand your topic, especially when people commonly misspell a word in your subject. Your title, thumbnail, description and the video itself matter far more. So treat tags as a small but useful finishing step, not a growth trick.

How to choose good tags

  1. Start with your main keyword. If your video is about making masala chai, your first tag should be exactly that phrase: "masala chai recipe".
  2. Add close variations. Think about how different people would search: "how to make masala tea", "indian chai at home".
  3. Use some longer phrases. A phrase of three or more words describes your video more precisely than a single broad word like "tea".
  4. Include real misspellings only if they are common. Do not invent them.
  5. Add your channel or series name if you make videos in a series.

How many tags to use

YouTube allows up to 500 characters in total. You do not need to fill all of it. Eight to fifteen relevant tags are usually enough. A few accurate tags are better than thirty loosely related ones.

Where to find tag ideas

  • Type your topic in the YouTube search box and note the autocomplete suggestions. They show what people actually search.
  • Look at the tags used by similar videos that perform well. Public tag viewers, including the Tag Finder in Trenndly, show them.
  • Read the comments on your own videos. Viewers often use the exact words they search with.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Misleading tags. Adding a popular name that has nothing to do with your video breaks YouTube's spam and deceptive practices policy and can hurt your channel.
  • Copying everything from a competitor. Use their tags as inspiration and keep only the ones that truly describe your video.
  • Tag stuffing. Repeating the same word in many forms adds nothing.

Quick checklist

  • First tag is your main keyword.
  • Every tag honestly matches the video.
  • Mix of short and long phrases.
  • Under 500 characters in total.

Tags will not make a weak video go viral, but accurate tags help YouTube understand your content, and that is worth two minutes at upload time.
