How to Find the Best Day and Time to Upload on YouTube

Trenndly Blog · 4 min read · Updated November 2026

Articles often claim there is one perfect day to upload. The truth is that it depends on your audience. A channel about exam preparation and a channel about weekend travel will have very different viewers. The good news is that you can find your own best time with free data.

Step 1: Check when your viewers are online

In YouTube Studio, open Analytics, go to the Audience tab and look for the "When your viewers are on YouTube" chart. It shows the days and hours with the most viewers. This is your starting point.

Step 2: Upload a little before the peak

Viewers need a short time for YouTube to process and begin showing your video. Publishing one to two hours before your audience's busiest period is a common approach.

Step 3: Compare your own videos

Look at your last ten to twenty videos. Note the day each was published and the views it got in the first 48 hours. If videos posted on one weekday regularly do better, that day is worth trying more often. The Channel Planner in Trenndly does this comparison for any public channel using the weekday of recent uploads.

Step 4: Be consistent

A steady schedule matters more than a perfect hour. If your viewers know you post every Saturday, they will start to come back on that day. Choose a schedule you can keep for months.

Step 5: Experiment for a month

Pick two different days or times and use each for about a month. Compare average views in the first two days. Change only one thing at a time, otherwise you will not know what caused the difference.

Things to remember

  • Time zones matter. If most viewers live in India, schedule in Indian time.
  • Video quality always matters more than upload time.
  • Small channels have little data, so be patient and keep testing.
