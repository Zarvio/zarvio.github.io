YouTube Hashtags: How Many to Use and Where to Put Them

Trenndly Blog · 4 min read · Updated November 2026

Hashtags are clickable words that start with the # sign. When a viewer taps one, YouTube shows other videos that use the same hashtag. Used well, they help viewers who browse a topic find your video.

Hashtags vs tags

Tags are hidden keywords in the upload settings. Hashtags are visible to viewers, either in the description or in the title. They serve different purposes, so you can use both.

Where to put them

  • In the description: add them at the end or the beginning. The first three hashtags from the description appear above your video title.
  • In the title: possible, but one at most is cleaner, and it uses up space for words that matter more.

How many to use

YouTube states that if a video has more than 15 hashtags, it ignores all of them for that video. A safe approach is three to five relevant hashtags. Since only the first three show above the title, put the most important ones first.

How to choose them

  1. Use one broad topic hashtag, for example #travel.
  2. Add one or two specific ones, for example #jaisalmer or #budgettravel.
  3. Optionally add a hashtag unique to your channel or series so viewers can find your other videos.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using trending hashtags that have nothing to do with your video. This can be treated as misleading.
  • Adding spaces. A hashtag is one word: #youtubetips, not #youtube tips.
  • Adding too many. More is not better.

Shorts

For YouTube Shorts, hashtags in the title or description help place the video with similar content. Keep them short and relevant here too.

Checklist

  • Three to five hashtags.
  • Most important ones first.
  • Every hashtag matches the video.
