Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Short, honest answers about how Trenndly works, what happens to your videos and what each tool does. If your question is not here, write to us on the Contact page.

Getting started

What is Trenndly?

Trenndly is a free set of tools for YouTube creators. You can generate tags, hashtags, keywords and title ideas from a video, check how strong your opening hook is, research competitor videos and channels, plan upload days, write scene-by-scene prompts for AI video tools, remove the visible watermark from your own Gemini or Veo videos, and create story prompts with Viral Stories.

Is Trenndly really free?

Yes. Every account gets free coins that refill every day. Each tool that uses AI costs a few coins, and the exact cost is shown in the side menu. Tag Finder does not use coins. We may show ads on some content pages to keep the service free.

Do I need an account?

You can open the site and try the tools as a guest a limited number of times. An account gives you daily coins, saves your History for 7 days and keeps the service secure against abuse. You can sign up with email or with Google.

Which devices and browsers work best?

Trenndly works on phones and computers. Watermark Remover runs inside your browser and needs a modern browser with video encoding support, so the latest Chrome or Edge on a computer gives the best result.

Your videos and privacy

What happens to my video after I upload it?

The video is used only to create your result and is deleted automatically after the analysis finishes. We do not keep your video file. For the hook check, only the first 10 seconds are used. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

Do you use my videos to train AI?

No. We do not use your videos to train our own models. Videos sent for analysis are processed by our AI provider under its own terms, and we delete them from the provider when the analysis is done.

Is Watermark Remover uploading my video?

No. Watermark Remover works inside your own browser on your own device. The video is not sent to our servers. When you leave the page, the cleaned video is gone from the browser, so download it first.

How do I delete my data or account?

You can delete any History item yourself. To delete your account and related data, email us from your account email address. The contact details are on the Contact page.

The tools

What does the Analyze tool give me?

You upload a video and get SEO-ready tags with a character counter, hashtags, keywords, five title ideas and an estimated SEO score. The score is a quick local estimate based on the quality and variety of the results. It is a guide, not a promise of ranking.

What does Suggestion (hook check) do?

It looks at the first 10 seconds of your video, scores your hook and explains what works and what to fix. If the hook is weak, it writes stronger opening lines in your video's language. Read our guide on writing a strong video hook to learn the ideas behind it.

What is Tag Finder and what are the other Competitor tools?

Tag Finder shows the public tags, description and stats of any YouTube video you paste. Compare Tags shows which tags a competitor uses that you do not. Channel Planner looks at a channel's recent videos to find which weekday gets the best average views. All of these use public data from the YouTube Data API.

What is Video Prompt?

You describe an idea, or add a video, and Trenndly writes a character image prompt plus scene-by-scene prompts that you can paste into AI image and video tools. Our guide on writing AI video prompts explains how good prompts are built.

What is Viral Stories?

You pick a story style, Trenndly writes five story ideas, and you choose one to turn into a character image prompt and scene-by-scene reference image and video prompts. You then use those prompts in the AI image and video tools of your choice.

What is Watermark Remover and what are its limits?

It removes the visible watermark from videos made with Gemini or Veo. It does not remove SynthID, the invisible mark AI tools add inside the video. Please use it only on videos you created or have permission to edit. See our guide on disclosing AI content on YouTube for the rules you should follow when you publish AI videos.

How do coins and Winter Spin work?

Daily coins refill every day (India time) and unused daily coins do not carry over. If an analysis fails on our side, the coins are returned. Winter Spin lets you spin once a day when it is available to win bonus coins, which stay in your account until you use them.

Results and expectations

Will Trenndly make my video go viral?

No tool can promise that. Trenndly gives you suggestions that help you present your video clearly. Views depend on your content, your audience and how viewers react to your video. Always read and edit the results before you publish.

Do tags still matter on YouTube?

Tags play a small role. YouTube has said they mostly help with common misspellings, and your title, thumbnail and first seconds matter far more. Our tags guide explains how to use them sensibly.

Can I trust the results without checking them?

AI results can contain mistakes. Check that every tag, title and claim matches your actual video, and do not use misleading metadata, because it can break YouTube's rules.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Use the Feedback button after you log in, or write to us from the Contact page. Tell us which tool you used and what happened.
