CQuestions and answers

Is Trenndly free to use?

Yes. Every account gets free coins that refill every day. Each tool uses a few coins and the cost is shown in the side menu. Tag Finder is free.

What happens to my video?

It is used only to create your result and is deleted automatically as soon as the analysis finishes. For Suggestion, only the first 10 seconds are used.

What does Suggestion do?

It scores your video's hook. If the score is below 90, it writes stronger opening lines in your video's language.

What are the Competitor tools?

Tag Finder shows the public tags, stats and description of any YouTube video. Compare tags shows which tags a competitor uses that you do not. Channel Planner shows the best days to upload.

What is Video Prompt?

Describe an idea, or add a video, and get a reference image prompt plus scene by scene prompts that you can paste into AI image and video tools.

Which videos can I upload?

Most common video formats work. The size limit is shown on the home page.

Why do I need to log in?

An account keeps the service secure, saves your History for 7 days and gives you your daily coins.

Will my video go viral?

Nobody can promise that. Trenndly gives you suggestions that help you present your video better. Always read and edit the results before you publish.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete any History item yourself. To delete your account and related data, email us at the address in the Contact section.

Privacy Policy

1About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Trenndly ("Trenndly", "we", "our" or "us") handles information when you use our website at trenndly.netlify.app and our tools: video tag and title analysis, Suggestion (hook check), Competitor tools (Tag Finder, tag comparison and Channel Planner), and Video Prompt.

By creating an account or using Trenndly, you confirm that you have read this policy and the User Rules in section 13. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

2Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address, username and profile photo, provided by you or by Google when you sign in with Google or with email and password.
  • Passwords are handled by our login provider. We never see or store your password in readable form.

Content you give us

  • Videos: a video you upload for analysis or as a reference for Video Prompt. For Suggestion, only the first 10 seconds of your video are used.
  • Text: ideas you type in Video Prompt, YouTube links or channel handles you paste in Competitor tools, and messages you send through Feedback.
  • Preview image: a small frame taken from your video, saved only so your History looks clear.

Results we create for you

  • Tags, hashtags, keywords, titles, SEO score, hook score, stronger hook lines, scene-by-scene prompts and reference image prompts.
  • Results from Tag Finder, tag comparison and Channel Planner, which use public data from the YouTube Data API.

Usage and technical information

  • Which tool you used, coin usage, file name, file size, whether it worked, and how long it took. We use this to run the service and fix problems.
  • A random visitor ID stored in your browser to count unique visitors once, your device type (mobile or desktop), and the date of first visit. If you log in, this ID is linked to your account.
  • Your IP address and browser type, which we use for security and abuse protection.

3How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, verify your email and let you log in.
  • To analyze your video or idea and give you the results you asked for.
  • To run the daily coin system and save your History for 7 days.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, automated misuse and security attacks.
  • To reply to your feedback and fix errors.
  • To understand overall usage so we can improve Trenndly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your videos to train our own models.

4Videos and AI processing

When you use Analyze, Suggestion or Video Prompt with a video, the video is sent securely to our AI provider so it can watch and listen to it and write your results. For Suggestion, only the first 10 seconds are used. Ideas you type are also processed by the AI provider to write your prompts.

We delete your video from the AI provider as soon as the analysis finishes, and we do not keep your video file ourselves. The AI provider handles information under its own terms and policies.

AI results can contain mistakes. Please read and edit every result before you publish it. Do not upload private, confidential or sensitive footage.

6Cookies and browser storage

Google AdSense may place cookies to show ads. You can manage ad personalization in Google Ads Settings. You can also clear storage and cookies from your browser settings at any time, but you may need to log in again.

7Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With the service providers listed in section 5, to run the service.
  • When the law requires it, or to respond to valid legal requests.
  • To investigate abuse, protect our users and service, or protect our rights.
  • If Trenndly is ever sold or merged, in which case we will give notice before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

10Your rights and choices

  • Deletion: delete any History item yourself, or email us to delete your account and related data.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using Trenndly at any time and clear your browser storage.
  • Complaints: contact us first, and you may also contact your local data protection authority.

These rights apply to you wherever you live, including under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and similar laws such as GDPR. To use a right, email us from your account email. We may need to verify that it is really you.

11Children's privacy

Trenndly is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Users under 18 should use Trenndly with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12International processing

Our providers run servers in different countries. This means your information may be processed outside the country where you live, including in countries whose data laws differ from yours. We choose reputable providers and use the protections they offer.

14Coins and fair use

  • Coins have no cash value and cannot be sold or transferred.
  • We may change coin amounts, prices, limits or features at any time, and the service may be unavailable for maintenance.
