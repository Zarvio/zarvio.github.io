1About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Trenndly ("Trenndly", "we", "our" or "us") handles information when you use our website at trenndly.me and our tools: video tag and title analysis, Suggestion (hook check), Competitor tools (Tag Finder, tag comparison and Channel Planner), Video Prompt, Watermark Remover, Viral Stories and Winter Spin.

By creating an account or using Trenndly, you confirm that you have read this policy and the User Rules in section 13. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

2Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address, username and profile photo, provided by you or by Google when you sign in with Google or with email and password.
  • Passwords are handled by our login provider. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Answers you give when you first sign up: your preferred language (English or Hindi), your age, and the goal you pick (for example, making hashtags or removing a watermark). We use these to personalize the guide you see.

Content you give us

  • Videos: a video you upload for analysis or as a reference for Video Prompt. For Suggestion, only the first 10 seconds of your video are used. A video you use in Watermark Remover is processed inside your browser and is not uploaded to us.
  • Text: ideas you type in Video Prompt, YouTube links or channel handles you paste in Competitor tools, and messages you send through Feedback.
  • Stories: the story style you pick and the story idea you choose in Viral Stories.
  • Preview image: a small frame taken from your video, saved only so your History looks clear.

Results we create for you

  • Tags, hashtags, keywords, titles, SEO score, hook score, stronger hook lines, scene-by-scene prompts and reference image prompts.
  • Story ideas, character image prompts, scene reference image prompts and scene video prompts from Viral Stories.
  • Results from Tag Finder, tag comparison and Channel Planner, which use public data from the YouTube Data API.
  • Our replies to your feedback, which you can read under Replies.

Usage and technical information

  • Which tool you used, coin usage, file name, file size, whether it worked, and how long it took. For Watermark Remover we record only whether it worked, any error message and the file size, not the video itself. We use this to run the service and fix problems.
  • Your Winter Spin activity, such as when you last spun and the coins you won.
  • A random visitor ID stored in your browser to count unique visitors once, your device type (mobile or desktop), and the date of first visit. If you log in, this ID is linked to your account.
  • Your IP address and browser type, which we use for security and abuse protection.

3How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, verify your email and let you log in.
  • To analyze your video or idea and give you the results you asked for.
  • To run the daily coin system and Winter Spin, and save your History for 7 days.
  • To personalize your first-time guide using your language, age and goal.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, automated misuse and security attacks.
  • To reply to your feedback and fix errors.
  • To understand overall usage so we can improve Trenndly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your videos to train our own models.

4Videos and AI processing

When you use Analyze, Suggestion or Video Prompt with a video, the video is sent securely to our AI provider so it can watch and listen to it and write your results. For Suggestion, only the first 10 seconds are used. Ideas you type and the story ideas you choose in Viral Stories are also processed by the AI provider to write your prompts.

We delete your video from the AI provider as soon as the analysis finishes, and we do not keep your video file ourselves. The AI provider handles information under its own terms and policies.

Watermark Remover is different. It cleans your video inside your own browser, on your own device. The video is not sent to our servers or to the AI provider. Your device can show you the cleaned video and a download button, and when you leave the page it is gone from the browser.

AI results can contain mistakes. Please read and edit every result before you publish it. Do not upload private, confidential or sensitive footage. Watermark Remover removes only the visible watermark. It does not remove SynthID, the invisible mark that AI tools add inside the video.

6Cookies and advertising

Browser storage

We use your browser's local storage (and similar technology) for things that make the site work:

  • Your theme choice (dark or light) and your last used tool.
  • A flag that you are logged in, so the page loads smoothly.
  • Your visitor ID, tutorial progress, language choice and the "What's new" notice.
  • Your last Competitor links, so you do not have to type them again.
  • The Viral Stories screen you were on, so a refresh brings you back to the same place.

Advertising and Google cookies

Trenndly uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can also clear storage and cookies from your browser settings at any time, but you may need to log in again.

10Your rights and choices
